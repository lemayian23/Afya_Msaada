# AfyaMsaada

AfyaMsaada is an Android-first substance-use information, early-support,
and referral platform designed to help people in Kenya access reliable
information and connect with appropriate professional support.

## Problem

People experiencing problems related to alcohol and other drug use may
struggle to identify reliable information or determine where to seek
appropriate professional support.

AfyaMsaada aims to reduce this gap by providing a simple mobile pathway
from information and self-screening guidance to verified support and
referral services.

## Core Features

- Substance-use education and awareness
- Non-diagnostic self-screening guidance
- Searchable support and treatment facilities
- County-based service discovery
- Direct access to the NACADA 1192 helpline
- Resources for people supporting someone experiencing substance-use problems
- Offline-first access to essential information

## Project Status

🚧 Early development

This project is currently an independent prototype inspired by real-world
substance-use prevention and support needs in Kenya. It is not an official
NACADA application unless formally adopted or authorized by NACADA.
